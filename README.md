# Retail-Sales-Dashboard 
  1. Project - <br>
  Retail Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI End-to-End Business Intelligence Project

  2. Description / Purpose - <br>
The Retail Sales Performance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution designed to monitor retail sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, regional trends, and time-based business metrics. It enables business users to make data-driven decisions through dynamic KPIs, advanced DAX measures, and interactive visualizations across five analytical dashboard pages.

3. Tech Stack - <br>
The dashboard was developed using the following technologies and tools: <br>
  * Power BI Desktop – Dashboard development & data visualization <br>
  * Power Query (M Language) – Data cleaning, transformation and ETL <br>
  * DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Business calculations and KPI measures <br>
  * Star Schema Data Modeling – Fact and Dimension table modeling <br>
  * CSV Dataset – Retail sales transaction data <br>
  * Microsoft Excel – Initial data exploration (optional) <br>
  * GitHub – Project documentation and portfolio hosting <br>

4. Data Source - <br>
Dataset: Retail Sales Dataset <br>
Source: Kaggle <br>
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/satyakidas07/retail-sales-dataset <br>

  Dataset Description - <br>
The dataset contains transactional retail sales data collected from a fictional retail business. Each record represents an individual customer order and includes customer demographics, product information, geographical details, pricing, discounts, profits, shipping information, customer ratings, and order status. <br>
 
  Data Modeling - <br>
The dataset was transformed into a Star Schema consisting of: <br>
* FactSales <br>
* DimCustomer <br>
* DimProduct <br>
* DimStore <br>
* DimDate <br>

5. Features / Highlights - <br>
* Business Problem <br>
Retail businesses generate thousands of sales transactions every month, making it difficult for management to quickly understand sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product profitability, regional performance, and seasonal trends using raw transactional data alone.
Without an interactive reporting system, decision-makers spend significant time preparing reports instead of analyzing business performance and identifying growth opportunities. <br>
* Goal of the Dashboard - <br>
The primary objective of this dashboard is to provide a centralized business intelligence solution that helps stakeholders: <br>
-- Monitor overall business performance<br>
-- Analyze sales and profit trends<br>
-- Identify top and low-performing products<br>
-- Understand customer purchasing behavior<br>
-- Evaluate regional sales performance<br>
-- Track monthly and yearly growth<br>
-- Support strategic business decisions through interactive insights<br>
* Dashboard Walkthrough - <br>
Page 1 – Executive Dashboard <br>
This page provides an overall snapshot of business performance using high-level KPIs and executive summaries.<br>
Page 2 – Product Analysis <br>
Focuses on product performance and category profitability.<br>
Page 3 – Customer Analysis <br>
Provides insights into customer demographics and purchasing behavior. <br>
Page 4 – Regional Analysis<br>
Analyzes geographical business performance across regions and cities.<br>
Page 5 – Time Analysis<br>
Provides time intelligence analysis using DAX measures.<br>

 * DAX Measures Used <br>
   * Total Sales
   * Total Profit
   * Profit Margin %
   * Total Orders
   * Total Quantity
   * Total Customers
   * Average Order Value
   * Average Profit per Order
   * Total Discount
   * Average Discount %
   * Average Shipping Cost
   * Average Customer Satisfaction
   * Return Orders
   * Return Rate %
   * Month-to-Date (MTD) Sales
   * Year-to-Date (YTD) Sales
   * Previous Year Sales
   * Sales Growth %
  
* Business Impact & Insights - <br>
This dashboard enables decision-makers to quickly identify business opportunities and operational challenges by providing a unified view of retail performance.<br>
Key business outcomes include:<br>
  * Monitor overall sales and profitability in real time.<br>
  * Identify top-performing and underperforming products for inventory and pricing decisions.<br>
  * Understand customer purchasing behavior to support retention and targeted marketing.<br>
  * Compare regional performance to optimize resource allocation and sales strategies.<br>
  * Track month-over-month and year-over-year growth using time-intelligence metrics.<br>
  * Evaluate return rates, discount trends, and customer satisfaction to improve operational efficiency.<br>
  * Empower managers with interactive filters and drill-down capabilities for faster, data-driven decision-making.<br>
